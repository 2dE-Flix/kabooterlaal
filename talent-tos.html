TERMS OF SERVICE — TALENTS

1. You Are an Independent Contractor

You are joining Ravie as an independent creator, not an employee. Any shadowbans, profile drops, or negative effects on your personal social media accounts are not Ravie's fault. You manage your own pages at your own risk.

2. Managing Your Own Taxes

Since you are not an employee, Ravie will not handle or pay your personal income tax. You are entirely responsible for filing your own government taxes on the money you earn from our network.

3. Absolute Secrecy

It is strictly forbidden to share, post, or leak Ravie's payout rules, internal guidelines, campaign briefs, or brand details anywhere online or in private chats. Sharing screenshots of our backend systems or chats will get you banned instantly and could lead to a lawsuit for leaking private business information.

4. Right Payment Details Required

To get paid, you must provide accurate bank or UPI details and real verification links. If you give us wrong accounts or broken links, your money will be stuck in the queue. We will only release your funds once your details are cleared and verified.

5. Missing Deadlines

Once a campaign officially ends, no extra or delayed payments will be given out. You must submit your video links and tracking data within the exact time limits written in the active brief. If you miss the deadline, you permanently lose the right to claim money for that project.

6. Changing Campaign Rules

Payout amounts, video styles, and delivery rules change depending on the brand and the budget. The unique rules for your cash milestones will be clearly shown inside each campaign brief. You agree to follow the specific rules of the campaign you click on and accept.

7. No Fake Views or Bots

If our team catches you using bots, fake traffic, view-buying services, or engagement groups to inflate your numbers, you will be banned instantly. Your entire balance will be frozen permanently, and we will seize all your earnings. We keep our platform 100% clean and real.

8. No Direct Contacting of Brands

Creators are strictly banned from contacting our brand clients directly to ask for extra money, free gifts, or separate side deals. Doing this bypasses our platform and ruins our business relationships. If you contact a brand behind our back, you will be removed instantly without pay.

9. Using Your Stats for Our Marketing

You give Ravie permission to use your channel name, profile picture, view stats, and campaign video snippets strictly for our own marketing pitches, case studies, and presentation decks to bring in new brands. We will never sell your face or content to outside companies for unrelated advertising.

10. Following Video Guidelines Exactly

You must create your assigned video content exactly according to the guidelines and script details inside the campaign file. If you miss a core talking point, use a forbidden layout, or show the wrong product, you will have to fix it at your own expense to get approved for payment.

11. Deleting Active Videos

If a social media platform removes your video, you must give us a valid reason, and we will deduct that slot from your balance. However, if you manually delete or archive an active campaign video yourself without our permission, you will be banned instantly and we reserve the right to take legal action for destroying brand assets.

12. Copyright Issues are Your Fault

If you use copyrighted music, stolen video clips, or unauthorized images that cause a copyright strike or lawsuit, you are entirely on your own. You must handle the blame and pay all related fines out of your own pocket. Ravie is fully insulated from your creative violations.

13. Rude or Toxic Behavior

Ravie reserves the right to remove any creator from our network at any point if your communication becomes toxic, rude, or unprofessional. We will also remove your profile if your content quality drops too low. We only work with polite, professional partners.

14. No Middleman Activities

You cannot pass our brand campaigns down to your friends, act as a secret middleman, or run your own mini-agency using our briefs. You must be the one creating the content. Anyone caught forwarding our campaign briefs to unverified users will be banned immediately.

15. No Group Disputes or Unions

If you have a problem with your payouts or your account, you must talk to our help desk alone as an individual user. Creators are strictly forbidden from forming groups, unions, or mass text threads to launch shared complaints against Ravie. We only solve account problems on a one-on-one basis.

16. Rule Updates

We can update these rules at any time. The new rules will only apply to future campaigns you accept. Any campaign you are currently working on will be completed under the rules that were active when you started it.
