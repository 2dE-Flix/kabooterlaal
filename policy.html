PRIVACY POLICY

1. Keeping Data Separate

We store brand business records and creator tracking files in completely separate databases to prevent leaks. No outside user can look across into the other data layer to view your private information.

2. Brand Data We Collect

For brands, Ravie collects company names, tax numbers, business banking details, campaign goals, product launch calendars, and the contact details of the managers running the project. This data is encrypted and only used to run your campaigns.

3. Creator Data We Collect

For creators, we collect your account profile details, social links, stats screenshots, view history, location markers, and payment details. We collect this strictly to check that your view milestones are real and to send your earnings.

4. Human Review Protection

All performance metrics, video links, and screenshots uploaded by creators are checked by hand by our internal team. This manual check layout is kept private so automated web scraper bots cannot download or steal your files. Your data sits safely inside our audited databases.

5. Local Law Rules

This Privacy Policy and your digital interactions with Ravie are controlled entirely by the local laws where the business owner runs the company. Any serious legal disputes about privacy rights must be brought only before the courts in our specific home territory.

6. Anonymous Case Studies

Ravie has the right to take your campaign view numbers, reach metrics, and performance charts to show them in our company case studies. However, we will completely erase and hide the real names of the brands and creators so your real identity stays a secret.

7. Historical Records

All active profiles and project records live on our secure servers. Even when a marketing campaign ends, we keep your performance numbers and account names safe inside our historical files for tax reporting and future network checkups.

8. Cloud Infrastructure Disclaimer

We run our tracking portals on major global cloud server systems to give you fast load speeds. If these external cloud hosts have an unexpected system blackout, crash, or server issue, Ravie cannot be blamed for any data lag or temporary downtime.

9. Security Cookies

Our website places small digital files called cookies on your computer when you log in. These cookies help us track your login location, verify your device, and spot bad actors trying to hack your profile. This is a pure security system built to keep our site safe.

10. No Selling or Trading Data

Ravie will never sell, rent, or trade your data to outside parties. Your private marketing briefs, banking tracks, and contact details will never be given to spam companies or digital ad brokers. Your files stay 100% inside our closed system.

11. Your Rights to Edit or Wipe Your Data

If you want to view the personal information we have stored, update it, or request a complete deletion of your profile, you can contact us via email. Our team will clear out your information where possible, except for data that is locked for ongoing campaigns or tax tracking.

12. Adult Creators Only (18+)

Our system is built strictly for creators who are 18 years of age or older. We do not collect or store files from anyone younger than 18. If our team finds out a creator lied about their age, we will purge their profile data instantly.

13. Emergency Security Freeze

If our security system notices an ongoing hacking attack, security loop issue, or system exploit, an automated circuit breaker will trigger a campaign freeze. This immediately locks down all data pathways to keep your corporate plans and personal information safe from thieves until the threat is dead.

14. Agreement by Using the Site

We can change or update these privacy rules at any time to protect against new security threats. We will show the newest updates on this page. By continuing to log into your account, open verification tickets, or run campaigns, you automatically agree to follow the latest rules.
