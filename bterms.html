TERMS OF SERVICE — BRANDS

1. 100% Upfront Payment

Brands must pay the full campaign budget before we start any work. We will not find creators or start any operations until the money actually clears and shows up in our bank account. A screenshot of a receipt or a transaction number does not count as payment.

2. Non-Refundable Money

Once your money is paid to Ravie, it cannot be refunded under any condition. If you cancel the project, pause the work, or change your mind later, we will not return the money. This capital is used instantly to reserve slots with our creators.

3. Total Control of Budget Distribution

Ravie has the absolute right to decide how your budget is divided among our creators. We track how well creators do and will shift money to better-performing creators if others fall behind. Brands do not get to pick which specific creator gets what portion of the funds.

4. Views and Reach Only

We only guarantee the video views or audience reach numbers written in your contract. We do not guarantee website clicks, product sales, or sign-ups. If a campaign hits its view goals but you do not make any sales, Ravie is not responsible.

5. No Blame for App Changes or Glitches

Ravie is not responsible for sudden updates, bugs, or algorithm changes on platforms like Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok. If an app changes its system and your views temporarily drop, our internal reports remain the final proof of our completed work.

6. Creator Bad Behavior

Creators are independent users and their mistakes, personal actions, or bad behavior are not the fault of Ravie. If a creator ruins a post or breaks our community rules, we will handle them by banning them from our network forever. The brand agrees not to blame or sue Ravie for the actions of individual creators.

7. Product and Link Liability

Brands are entirely responsible for the products, links, and guidelines they give us to promote. If your company provides a bad product, illegal items, or misleading information that causes legal trouble, the brand must handle all fines and legal costs. Ravie will not take the blame for your brand assets.

8. Strict 48-Hour Review Window

Brands have exactly 48 hours to check the final performance reports we send and raise any questions or disputes. If you do not complain within this two-day window, the data is locked. After 48 hours, the project is officially finished and cannot be challenged.

9. Our Responsibility Limit

If a legal mediator finds that Ravie made a major mistake that caused actual harm, the maximum amount of money we will ever pay you back is limited to our personal agency profit from that specific campaign. This completely excludes any money that was already paid out to creators.

10. Deleting Unsafe Videos

Ravie keeps the right to instantly delete or archive any campaign video if we see that a creator's post breaks social app rules or brings immediate legal threats. This defensive action will not count as a broken promise or a failed delivery to the brand.

11. Final Agreement

This document is the final and only binding agreement between Ravie and your brand. It cancels and replaces all past phone calls, email threads, text messages, or pitch decks shared before. No informal messages have any legal power compared to these points.

12. Private Legal Settlement

If a dispute happens between Ravie and a brand, neither side is allowed to go to a public government court. The issue must be settled quietly using a private, independent mediator in our home city. This keeps business details safe, private, and fast.

13. Rule Updates

We can update these rules at any time. When we update them, we will post the new version here. The new rules will only apply to future campaigns. Any campaign that is already active will finish under the rules that were in place when it started.
